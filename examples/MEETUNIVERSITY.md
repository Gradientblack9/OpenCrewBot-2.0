## Class 11 campaign

**Audience.** Class 11 students exploring subject and career options, and parents helping them choose next steps. CBSE publishes senior-secondary skill curricula across several fields. A student's available options depend on their school. ASER 2023 provides additional context for rural youth aged 14–18. Treat interest in short exercises and parent sessions as hypotheses to test with the intended schools. [CBSE skill education curriculum](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/skill-education-curriculum.html) · [ASER 2023 Beyond Basics](https://asercentre.org/aser-2023-beyond-basics/).

**Positions.** For students, sell a manageable month of exploration with small tasks and a personal shortlist. For parents and schools, show a visible decision process with discussion prompts, school-verified options and an adviser follow-up.

**Creative concepts.** *Try three futures* uses short projects to sample three interests. *One page, next steps* turns the experience into a plan students can take to a family or school discussion.

**Draft carousel copy.** Slide 1 “Try three futures this month.” Slide 2 “Pick one thing you enjoy doing and one thing you want to learn.” Slide 3 “Set a 15-minute challenge for each interest. Sketch a design, interpret a chart or interview someone about their work.” Slide 4 “Write down which task you would do again. Ask your school about subjects that let you do more of it.” Slide 5 “Save this worksheet and bring it to your next counselling conversation.”

**Draft parent email.** Subject “Help your Class 11 student make a one-page plan.” Body “This week, ask what your student enjoyed trying and which options they want to explore next. Use our free worksheet to record two options and one question to ask their school. Join the parent Q&A if you would like help structuring that conversation.”

Measure worksheet completions among recipients, parent Q&A attendance among registrants and adviser bookings among completers. Compare by channel and school cohort so a large social audience does not conceal a school workshop's conversion rate.

Qualification is the largest loss by headcount and proportion, with 426 of 700 leads lost at that stage. Paid search qualifies 20.0% of its leads, compared with 60.0% for counsellor referrals. School workshops generate the most sales and revenue. Test a narrower paid-search landing page against the current page and measure cost per qualified lead. Pilot a structured referral handoff and track qualified leads and sales per active counsellor. The sample lacks spend, dates and attribution detail, which are needed for ROI and causal comparisons.
